# Roadmap

E.V. V1 is out: a private, fully local voice assistant that talks, controls your PC, and remembers you, all in a cyber HUD. Here is the direction for V2. No timelines, just where it's going.

## Coming in V2

- 👁️ **Sees your screen, still 100% local.** E.V. will understand what's actually on your screen and act on it, without anything leaving your machine.
- ✋ **Interrupt anytime.** Stop or redirect E.V. mid-task, including by voice, the moment you want.
- 🌙 **Works quietly in the background.** Ask for something, E.V. handles it without hijacking your screen, and shows you the result when you ask.
- 🗣️ **Understands you more naturally.** Less "say the exact phrase," more "just talk to it."
- 🧠 **Remembers more, wanders less.** Longer, more coherent conversations that hold on to what matters.
- 📦 **One-click install.** No more manual setup.
- 🛠️ **Does more, on your machine.** More real tasks handled locally.

Still local-first, still free by default, still private. Stars and ideas welcome, and if you forked it, contributions too. 🕸️

Have a request or an idea? Open an issue or a discussion.
